Northstar Studio Employee Handbook

Northstar Studio's normal working hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Everyone should keep their work calendar up to date and let their manager know if they will be unavailable.

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation requests should be submitted to the manager at least two weeks in advance. Up to five unused vacation days may be carried into the next year. Employees who are sick should notify their manager before the start of their workday. The company provides 10 paid sick days each year.

The company observes New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day as paid holidays. If a holiday falls on a weekend, the company will announce which weekday will be observed.

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business travel, meals, and work-related supplies. Receipts are required for expenses over $25. Expense reports should be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days. Ask your manager for approval before booking travel.

The company provides a laptop and the software needed for each employee's role. Company devices should be used responsibly and returned when employment ends. For technical help, contact the IT team at it@northstar.example. For questions about pay, benefits, or leave, contact People Operations at people@northstar.example.

Employees should report workplace safety concerns or inappropriate conduct to their manager or People Operations. Reports will be handled as privately as possible.
